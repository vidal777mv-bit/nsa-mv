The Martian map

Where Earth runs out.

  • 4.6% of 1,838 measured Mars places have no Earth twin (95% interval 3.7–5.6%).
  • Adding Arctic rock (60–84° N, ice left out) brought the Mars gap from 11.7% to 4.6%, and Moon sites without a twin from 3 to 1.
  • Where there is no twin, rehearsal on Earth is not possible. Our reading: robots go first.

Honest by design

A modest signal, reported as it came out.

  • Both pre-registered tests narrowly failed. v1.3 blind holdout (15 regions): AUC 0.695 (needed 0.70), nearest region 6 of 15 (needed 7).
  • The signal is real (permutation p 0.007) but modest. It measures operability, not geological origin.
  • Every number is generated from a facts ledger; none is typed by hand.
